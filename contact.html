Common questions

How long does a full project take?

Allow two to three weeks for drawings, then six to ten weeks in the workshop once the drawings are approved. We’ll confirm a date when you commission.

Can I supply my own timber?

Often, yes. We’ll look at the boards first to check species, moisture, dimensions and condition before agreeing that they’re suitable.

Can I choose the materials and finishes?

That’s most of the fun. Timber tone, fabrics, leather and finish are all chosen with you, from samples, before we draw the final piece.

Do you work with clients outside Rotterdam?

Yes. When a studio visit isn’t possible we work by video call and share drawings and samples digitally for approval.

Do you ship?

Within the Benelux we deliver, place and level the piece ourselves. Further afield, pieces are crated, insured and sent by freight.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit confirms your order and the balance is due before delivery or collection.
