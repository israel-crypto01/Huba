Process

From a first conversation to a signed card on the underside: four stages, no surprises.

( 01 )

Conversation

  • We start with the room, not the piece: ceiling height, afternoon light, how many sit down to dinner.
  • Bring photos, floor plans, or nothing at all.
  • Free, about an hour, at the studio or by video call.
No commitment
( 02 )

Drawings

  • You receive hand drawings at 1:10, a materials board and a fixed price.
  • Two rounds of revisions are included.
  • The drawing fee is credited in full if you go ahead, and the drawings are yours to keep if you don’t.
2 – 3 weeks
( 03 )

The making

  • Timber is chosen board by board, milled, then left to settle for a fortnight.
  • Joinery, shaping, sanding to 400 grit and four hand-rubbed coats of oil.
  • You get photos at each milestone. A 50% deposit is due at the start.
6 – 10 weeks
( 04 )

Delivery

  • In the Benelux we deliver, level the piece and take the packaging away. Further afield it is crated and insured.
  • Every piece arrives with a tin of finishing oil, care notes and a signed card with the date it left the bench.
  • The balance is due on delivery, and repairs are done at cost for life.
Lifetime repair

Things we insist on

( A )

Air-dried timber only

Kiln-dried stock moves less, but it works dead. We would rather wait for the real thing.

( B )

Joints cut, not fastened

Mortise and tenon, dovetail or wedge. Metal fixings only where a piece has to come apart.

( C )

Oil, never lacquer

A lacquered surface fails all at once. An oiled one can be repaired with a cloth.

( D )

Signed underneath

Maker, date and timber source are burned into the underside of every piece.
