Process

From a first conversation to a signed card on the underside: four stages, no surprises.

( 01 )

Conversation

  • We start with the room, not the piece: ceiling height, afternoon light, how many sit down to dinner.
  • Bring photos, floor plans, or nothing at all.
  • Free, about an hour, at the studio or by video call.
No commitment

Delivery

  • The balance is due on delivery, and repairs are done at cost for life.
Lifetime repair

Things we insist on

( A )

Air-dried timber only

Kiln-dried stock moves less, but it works dead. We would rather wait for the real thing.

( B )

Joints cut, not fastened

Mortise and tenon, dovetail or wedge. Metal fixings only where a piece has to come apart.

( C )

Oil, never lacquer

A lacquered surface fails all at once. An oiled one can be repaired with a cloth.

( D )

Signed underneath

Maker, date and timber source are burned into the underside of every piece.
