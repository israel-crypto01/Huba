The studio

Designed and built in our workshop in Rotterdam.

Two makers, one bench room, and a rule we have never broken: nothing leaves the workshop that we wouldn’t keep for ourselves.

It started with
a chair.

Furna opened in 2014 in a former shipyard shed in Rotterdam. We took on the jobs bigger makers turned down: odd dimensions, difficult rooms, single pieces. That constraint became the practice.

The shed is still here. Today we build around forty commissions a year, each drawn from scratch and signed and dated on the underside.

How we work

( A )

One client at a time

We keep no more than four commissions open at once, so our answers stay honest and our timelines stay realistic.

( B )

Solid wood, visible joints

No veneer over chipboard and no hidden fixings. You can see how every piece is put together.

( C )

Repairable forever

Any Furna piece can come back in any year to be refinished or rebuilt, at cost.
